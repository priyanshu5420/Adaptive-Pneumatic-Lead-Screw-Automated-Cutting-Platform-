# Adaptive Pneumatic–Lead-Screw Automated Cutting Platform

## Project Overview

The **Adaptive Pneumatic–Lead-Screw Automated Cutting Platform** is an academic prototype developed for controlled and programmable cutting of **wood and metal laboratory specimens**.

The project evolved from an initial soft-robotic concept and was later redesigned into a laboratory specimen cutting system using a **motorized cutting mechanism, horizontal lead-screw motion system, and 3D-printed components**.

## Project Objective

The main objective was to develop a compact cutting platform capable of:

- Controlled and programmable cutting motion
- Specific-distance and parallel cutting
- Cutting wood and metal specimens
- Modular mechanical design and prototyping
- Safe and easy-to-use operation

## Lead-Screw Mechanism

The lead-screw mechanism converts the **rotary motion of the stepper motor into controlled linear motion**.

The mechanism was designed as a **2-DOF system** to enable parallel and specific-distance movement of the cutting arm.

### Design Parameters

- Crank Length: **3 cm**
- Coupler Length: **5 cm**
- Slider Length: **7 cm**
- Stroke Length: **6 cm**
- Minimum DC Motor Torque: **3.54 Nm**

## Hardware Used

- ESP32
- NEMA17 Stepper Motor
- A4988 Stepper Motor Driver
- MG995R Servo Motor
- High-Torque DC Motor
- Mechanical Relay
- Joystick Module
- I2C Display

## CAD & Prototyping

Mechanical components were designed and dimensioned using **SolidWorks**.

Selected components were **3D printed for rapid prototyping**, while the lead screw was used for load-bearing and precision linear motion.

The electronic connections were planned using **Fritzing**.

## Design Evolution

The initial concept involved a **soft robotic gripper and robotic arm for automated food cutting**.

Due to a change in the overall project concept, the design was later shifted toward a **laboratory specimen cutting platform for wood and metal**.

The final design replaced the earlier food-cutting concept with a motorized cutting head and lead-screw based linear movement system.
